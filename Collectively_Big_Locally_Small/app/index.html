GHB Simulation Lab

How hospitals of every size can learn from each other without sharing a single patient record. Set up a scenario and test it in a minute.

Companion to Borrowing Strength Without Borrowing Data, the Graph Hierarchical Borrowing (GHB) paper, and to the survey Collectively Big, Locally Small: measure smallness, watch GHB and GHB-lite move information between hospitals over a sparse graph with differential privacy and no server, test them against every method in the paper, and read the real-data results.
